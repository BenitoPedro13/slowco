First version, 2026-11-09 (`tasks/TASK-definition.md`). Owns: the idea, essence, audiences,
personality, the line bank, voice rules and claim discipline. Everything here is **provisional
until the client confirms it** (`08` #16); where a rule is our proposal it says **[confirm]**.

## 1. The idea

### 1.1 What the brand already says about itself

- The founding sentence is the client's own: **"Devagar e sempre é a visão que nos guia"**
  (Reel 2024-06-25). "Slow" is not speed, it is a stance: steady, step by step, from São Gonçalo
  outwards ("De São Gonçalo pro mundo", 2021-06-26; "DAS RUAS PARA O MUNDO", Reel 2024-08-25).
- **Slow Mode** is the brand's recurring device: a state you enter ("SEJA BEM VINDO AO SLOW
  MODE", 2025-08-21), that people "activate" ("Vetin ativou o Slow Mode", 2025-09-03), that is
  "real" ("O SLOW MODE É REAL", Reel 2025-08-15). It is printed on the 2025 shorts and jersey.
- The garments carry a **legal-label voice**: "SLOW CREATIVE STUDIO AND INDUSTRY APPAREL",
  "FOR PROFESSIONAL USE ONLY. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED." (2021-08-13). Deadpan, industrial, a joke
  told with a straight face.
- It sells by **drops**: a dated teaser, then "DISPONÍVEL AGORA" (`00` §B.2).

### 1.2 Working concept (internal, not public copy)

> **Entrar no Slow Mode.** The site is the place where you switch into Slow Mode: the visitor
> literally changes the mode of the page (the theme switch, `04` §3), follows the current drop,
> and leaves through WhatsApp with the pieces in the bag.

### 1.3 How the concept steers the three signature moments

| Moment | How it carries the idea |
| --- | --- |
| Hero + theme switch | switching modes is the first thing you can do; the giant SLOW is the stage |
| Drop mode | the brand's real rhythm: teaser with date and hour, then "DISPONÍVEL AGORA", then archive |
| Bag counter | the bag is the only cart; it counts up like a label number, not like a shop widget |

## 2. Essence

**Streetwear de São Gonçalo e do Rio, feito devagar e sempre, em drops.** Black and white
garments, one accent per collection, worn by the scene that makes it (artists, friends, members).

Three words for designers (internal): **firme, local, sem pressa**.

## 3. Audiences

| Audience | What they need from the site | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |

## 4. Personality

| Is | Is not |

| confident, loud when announcing, short | corporate, explanatory, "nossa missão é…" |
| proud of São Gonçalo, Rio, favela, Brazil | touristic, exotic, "vibe carioca" clichés |

## 6. Voice rules

### 6.1 Language and person

1. pt-BR. Talk to the visitor as **você**, mostly in the imperative ("Escolha o tamanho").
2. English only where the brand already uses it: names (Slow Mode, Slow Racing Club, drop names),
   the legal-label lines L1/L2, L13, L15, L16. Never new English UI copy.
3. "Drop" stays "drop" (the brand's word); "sacola", not "carrinho" (OUTFIT's "Bag" is theirs;
   "sacola" is the pt-BR store word) **[confirm]**.

### 6.2 Case

4. **Caps** for announcements, drop states, CTAs, the legal-label lines and navigation: `DISPONÍVEL
   AGORA`, `PEDIR NO WHATSAPP`. Stored in caps in `05`, never produced by `text-transform` (screen
   readers and copy-paste get what is shown).
5. **Sentence case** for running text, helper text, errors and alt text.
6. Handles lowercase (`@slow_tm`), never inside a caps block's `text-transform`.

### 6.3 Rhythm and punctuation

7. Short: a CTA is 1–3 words; a sentence is at most ~14 words.
8. No exclamation marks in UI copy (the captions use "‼️"; the site's loudness comes from type
   size, not punctuation). No ellipsis teasers.

### 6.4 Dates, times, prices, numbers

9. Dates `24/08/2025` (or `24/08` when the year is obvious), times `18h` / `00h`, weekday in full
   when used: `domingo, 24/08, às 00h`. Never "ÁS".
10. Prices, when the client gives them: `R$ 199,90` (space after R$, comma decimal). Never shown
    without a client source (§7).
11. Sizes as the client labels them (`P`, `M`, `G`, `GG`? or `S`–`XL`?), **open** (`08` #7).

### 6.5 Emoji

12. **No emoji in site UI or in the WhatsApp message** (screen readers read them aloud, and the
    site's tone is in the type). Instagram keeps its emoji. **[confirm]**

### 6.6 Do / don't, from the real captions

| Do (site) | Don't | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DISPONÍVEL AGORA` | `DISPONÍVEL AGORA 🪖` | §6.5 |
| `domingo, 24/08, às 00h` | `DOMINGO 24/08 ÁS 00:00H` | §6.4 rule 9, typo |
| `CARGA NOVA` + date from the drop record | `CARGA NOVA HOJE 16H` hard-coded | "hoje" goes stale; the state comes from data (`04` §5) |
| `Peças limitadas` only on a drop the client marked limited | `Peças limitadas e sem reposição` on every page | claim (§7) |
| `PEDIR NO WHATSAPP` | `COMPRAR AGORA`, `FINALIZAR COMPRA` | nothing is bought on the site; the order is a message (§7) |
| `Seja bem-vindo ao Slow Mode` (L8, approved) | `Bem-vindo à melhor marca do Rio` | invented superlative |
| `@slow_tm` | `@SLOW_TM`, `Slow_TM` | rule 6 |
| `Esgotado` + struck-through size + text | a greyed-out size only | colour never alone (`04` §8) |

### 6.7 The name

13. Running text: **Slow Company** (as the profile spells it, `08` #1). Display: **SLOW** (the
    wordmark word). "Slow TM" is only the handle.
14. The accessible name of any wordmark (stand-in or client file) is `Slow Company`.

## 7. Claim discipline

### 7.1 Three classes

| Class | Examples | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fact** | price, size, stock, drop date/hour, availability, payment, shipping, exchange terms, phone number, partner names | a client source for this release, recorded in `05` |
| **Brand line** | L1–L18 | client approval of the bank (`08` #16) |
| **New UI copy** | `PEDIR NO WHATSAPP`, `Escolha o tamanho` | Claude proposal, client approval (`08` #16); makes no claim |

### 7.2 Rules

15. No price, size, stock, date, shipping, payment or exchange term without a client source.
    Missing → the element is not rendered (`04` §9), never a placeholder like "R$ XX".
16. The site never says an order is **confirmed**: the WhatsApp message is a request; stock and
    payment are confirmed in the chat. Copy says "pedir", never "comprar"/"finalizar compra".
17. "Limitado", "sem reposição", "exclusivo", "pré-venda": per drop, only when the client marks it.
18. Named people, artists and partners appear only with the client's confirmation (`08` #9).
19. No international shipping implied by "worldwide" (L15).
20. No superlatives the client didn't say ("a maior", "a melhor", "original" as a quality claim).
    "Recuse imitações" (L10) is the client's own and may stay.

## 8. Quick checklist for any public string

1. Is it in `05` with an ID and a source?
2. Is every fact sourced from the client for this release?
3. Caps vs sentence case per §6.2, stored as shown?
4. No emoji, no exclamation mark, no "comprar"?
5. Would it still be true next week (no "hoje", no "últimas peças" from code)?
